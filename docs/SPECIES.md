## Stat block (every sentient race)

These columns become the creator's stat block:

| Stat | Meaning (proposed) |
|---|---|
| Size | Tiny · Small · Medium · Large · Giant |
| Classification | Broad kind (see grouping below) |
| Health | Toughness: how much harm it takes to bring one down |
| Natural Armor | Hide, scales, bark, stone |
| Strength · Agility · Constitution | Body |
| Willpower · Intelligence · Wisdom | Mind (Intelligence also drives learning and technology) |
| Mana | Magical capacity |
| Stamina | How long it can keep going (marching, working) |
| Speed · Flying · Climbing · Leaping · Swimming | Movement (0 = cannot) |
| Total | Sum of Health through Swimming (worked out automatically) |
| Fighting age · Lifespan | When it can fight; how long it lives |
| Affinity · Secondary affinity | Magic affinities |
| Specialty skills | Unique racial abilities |

These numbers give the base stats for soldiers and heroes (8.2, section 6 of the creator), and feed travel (speed, flying, climbing, swimming).

## Draft grouping into species (guess, to be confirmed)

The sheet's **Classification** looks like a broad *kind* (Mammalian, Reptilian, Fay...), too broad to be a species (bear-folk and squirrel-folk can't be one species). Proposal: Classification = **kind** (shared traits); entries that are clearly variants of one people = **races of one species**.

|---|---|
